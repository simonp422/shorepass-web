The problem

Beach passes
are stuck in 1995.

Every summer, millions of beach-goers deal with the same friction. It doesn't have to be this way.

🏷️

Lost tags, wasted trips

You drove an hour to the beach only to realize your tag is still on last summer's bag. Now you're in line at town hall — on a Saturday in July.

📋

Manual enforcement gaps

Beach officers spend their whole shift visually checking tags. Fakes and forgeries go undetected. It's a system built on the honor system.

📊

Zero visibility for anyone

Municipalities can't see real-time visitor data. Beach-goers can't buy a day pass at 7am without waiting for an office to open.

How it works

Three steps.
You're in.

ShorePass removes every friction point between you and the sand.

1

Open the app, pick your pass

Find your beach, choose daily or seasonal, and pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay, or any card. Done in 30 seconds — even on the way there.

2

Your pass lives in your phone

No plastic tag. No paper. Your pass is stored in ShorePass — or added to your Apple Wallet or Google Wallet for offline access, always.

3

Scan and walk straight in

The officer scans your QR code. Green means go. Faster than any tag check you've ever seen — and 100% forgery-proof.

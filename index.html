How it works

Beach access in
three taps.

We removed every friction point between you and the sand. No offices. No cash. No stress.

1

Pick your beach & your pass

Open ShorePass, find your beach from the list, and choose a daily, weekend, or full season pass — whatever you need.

2

Pay in seconds, keep it forever

Apple Pay, Google Pay, or any card. Your pass lives in the ShorePass app — or add it to your Apple or Google Wallet so it works even offline.

3

Scan and walk straight in

Show your QR code at the beach entrance. The officer scans it, you're in. Takes less than 2 seconds. No tag needed, ever again.
